# Brand Guidelines & Visual Identity
## AI Smart Study Planner

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Aesthetic Core:** High-focus academic sanctuary; clean architectural typography; disciplined organic forest green accents.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Personality

The AI Smart Study Planner is designed to feel like an authoritative academic partner—calm, precise, dignified, and encouraging. It purposefully avoids the hyperactive, cartoonish gamification of language apps and the soulless, generic templates of typical SaaS tools.

### Brand Attributes:
- **Intellectual & Focused:** Every pixel respects cognitive bandwidth. Zero distracting animations or sensory overload.
- **Precise & Reliable:** Schedules are mathematically sound; metrics reflect empirical progress.
- **Warm & Scholarly:** Warm paper-tinted surfaces (`#FAF9F6`) in light mode; deep slate/graphite (`#121316`) in dark mode.
- **Grounded Tone of Voice:** Encouraging without patronizing. Objective, clear, and actionable feedback.

---

## 2. Logo & Brand Mark

- **Iconography:** The brand symbol represents the convergence of an open geometric book and a celestial orbital node—symbolizing disciplined knowledge acquisition and spatial syllabus clarity.
- **Wordmark:** Set in `Inter` SemiBold (`letter-spacing: -0.025em`) with "Smart" highlighted in subtle forest green.

---

## 3. Color Codes & Usage Ratios

| Swatch | Name | Light Mode | Dark Mode | Canvas Ratio | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas** | Alabaster / Slate | `#FAF9F6` | `#121316` | 60% | Main workspace background |
| **Surface** | Crisp Paper / Elevated Graphite | `#FFFFFF` | `#1A1C20` | 25% | Cards, sidebars, active study panels |
| **Borders** | Fine Separator | `#E8E7E1` | `#2A2E36` | 5% | 1px clean architectural structural dividers |
| **Text Primary** | Deep Charcoal / Crisp Off-White | `#191C1D` | `#EDEFEF` | 7% | High-contrast readable typography |
| **Accent** | Forest Focus Green / Luminous Sage | `#2D5A27` | `#5B8C51` | 3% | Primary CTAs, active pills, mastery highlights |

---

## 4. Imagery & Visual Assets

- **No Stock Photos:** Avoid generic stock photos of smiling students in libraries.
- **No Neon Gradients:** Zero purple-to-cyan gradient meshes.
- **Procedural 3D Visuals:** Use Three.js to render authentic, mathematical representations of the user's actual curriculum topology (Study Universe) and ambient progress orb.
